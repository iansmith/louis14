## The Bug
**Test:** `nested-after-float-clearance`
**Current diff:** 0.5% (2500/480000 pixels, max diff: 255)
**Priority:** High (P21-blocker)

**Pattern:** `columns:4; height:100; width:100` with `float:left; height:200` followed by `clear:left; columns:2; height:200`. Float + clear inside multicol — the float over-paints past the 100px outer when the unconditional clip is removed.

The float's margin/position may not be correctly constrained by the column fragmentainer, and the clear element may not correctly account for the float's extent.
