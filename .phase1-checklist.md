## Blink AppliedTextDecoration class (lines 18-48 / typedef line 50)

Fields (verbatim per WebFetch of pinned SHA):
- `unsigned lines_ : kTextDecorationLineBits;` — bitfield of underline | overline | line-through.
- `unsigned style_ : 3;` — `ETextDecorationStyle` (solid/double/dotted/dashed/wavy).
- `Color color_;` — the resolved color (may be `currentcolor`).
- `TextDecorationThickness thickness_;` — `{ auto | from-font | <length-percentage> }`.
- `Length underline_offset_;` — offset relative to the underline baseline.

Public accessors: `Lines()`, `Style()`, `GetColor()`, `SetColor()`, `Thickness()`, `UnderlineOffset()`.

`using AppliedTextDecorationVector = GCedHeapVector<AppliedTextDecoration, 1>;`

Style-builder behavior: when an element establishes a text decoration (line ≠ none),
the builder *appends* a new `AppliedTextDecoration` to the inherited vector. A
descendant cannot remove an ancestor's entry. `text-decoration-line: none` simply
contributes nothing.
